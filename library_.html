Chapter 5: The Soft Zone

My whole life I have worked on building a resilient approach to the learning process. When I think about creativity, it is always in relation to a foundation.

The learning principle is to plunge into the detailed mystery of the micro in order to understand what makes the macro tick. We are bombarded with more and more information on television, radio, and the internet.

In my experience, successful people shoot for the stars, put their hearts on the line in every battle, and ultimately discover that the lessons learned from the pursuit of excellence mean much more than the immediate trophies and glory.
